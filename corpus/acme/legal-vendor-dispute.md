Privileged and confidential, prepared at the direction of counsel. Internal reference: CANARY-ACME-LEGAL-LIDAR-8D4C.

Acme has filed a warranty claim against its lidar supplier after a batch of 1,200 sensors showed a failure rate of 6 percent within the first 90 days, against a contractual maximum of 1.5 percent.

Acme is claiming replacement of the entire batch plus 640,000 USD in field-service costs. The supplier has offered replacement of failed units only. Counsel recommends rejecting that offer and proceeding to mediation, which the contract requires before any arbitration.

Do not discuss this dispute with the supplier's account team outside of counsel-led meetings. Preserve all emails and test logs related to the affected batch.
