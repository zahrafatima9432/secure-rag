Confidential. These bands apply to all new offers and annual adjustments from 1 April 2026. Internal reference: CANARY-ACME-HR-BANDS-7A3D.

Software engineering bands: Engineer I ranges from 82,000 to 98,000 USD; Engineer II from 98,000 to 121,000 USD; Senior Engineer from 121,000 to 152,000 USD; Staff Engineer from 152,000 to 188,000 USD.

Robotics field technicians range from 54,000 to 71,000 USD, with a 12 percent shift differential for night coverage.

Offers above the band maximum need written approval from the CFO. Annual merit increases are budgeted at 4.5 percent of payroll for 2026, with the top performance tier eligible for up to 9 percent.
