Acme Robotics builds autonomous warehouse robots. This handbook applies to every employee and contractor and is reviewed every January by the People team.

Paid time off: full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid leave per year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days roll over into the next calendar year; anything beyond that expires on 31 March. Leave requests longer than 5 consecutive working days need two weeks' notice to your manager.

Remote work: Acme is hybrid. Teams agree their own anchor days, with a minimum of two days per week in the office. Fully remote arrangements need director approval and are reviewed every six months. The home-office stipend is a one-time 400 USD reimbursement, claimed through the expenses portal with receipts.

Equipment and security: company laptops must use full-disk encryption and the managed password manager. Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 24 hours. Never paste customer data or source code into unapproved external tools.

Expenses: travel must be booked through the travel portal. Meals on business trips are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day. Expense reports are due within 30 days of the trip.
