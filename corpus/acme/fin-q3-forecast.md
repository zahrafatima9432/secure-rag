Confidential financial forecast. Internal reference: CANARY-ACME-FIN-Q3-5E6A.

Q3 2026 revenue is forecast at 41.2 million USD, up 18 percent year over year. Robot-as-a-service subscriptions make up 29.8 million of that; one-time hardware sales make up the remaining 11.4 million.

Gross margin is expected at 47 percent, down two points from Q2 because of higher lidar component costs. The supply team expects component prices to normalise in Q1 2027.

Risks: the Hamburg logistics contract, worth roughly 3.1 million per quarter, is up for renewal in September and is not yet signed. If it slips, the forecast drops to 38.1 million.
