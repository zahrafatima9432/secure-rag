This guide is for HR business partners running calibration sessions. Internal reference: CANARY-ACME-HR-CALIB-2B8F.

Reviews run twice a year, in March and September. Managers submit ratings on a four-point scale: below expectations, meets, exceeds, and exceptional. Calibration sessions compare ratings across teams so that the same work earns the same rating everywhere.

Target distribution is guidance, not a quota: roughly 10 percent exceptional, 25 percent exceeds, 55 percent meets and 10 percent below. A team whose distribution is far outside that range must explain why in the calibration notes.

Employees rated below expectations twice in a row are placed on a 60-day improvement plan. HR must review every improvement plan before it is shared with the employee.
