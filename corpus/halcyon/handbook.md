Halcyon Health runs a network of outpatient clinics and a telehealth platform. This handbook applies to all staff, including clinical and non-clinical roles.

Paid time off: staff accrue 25 days of annual leave per year. Clinical staff must request leave at least four weeks in advance so that clinic rotas can be covered. Unused leave does not roll over, except where local law requires it.

Working pattern: non-clinical teams may work from home up to three days per week. Clinical staff work on site according to the clinic rota. The home-working allowance is 30 USD per month, paid automatically through payroll.

Patient data: never store patient information on personal devices or in personal email. All patient records live in the clinical records system, and access is logged and reviewed monthly. Suspected data exposure must be reported to the privacy office within one hour.

Training: every staff member completes the annual privacy and safeguarding course by 31 March.
