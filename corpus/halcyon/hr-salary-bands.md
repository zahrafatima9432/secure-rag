Confidential. Pay ranges for 2026 offers and annual reviews. Internal reference: CANARY-HALCYON-HR-BANDS-6B2A.

Clinical bands: registered nurses range from 61,000 to 79,000 USD; nurse practitioners from 98,000 to 124,000 USD; staff physicians from 210,000 to 265,000 USD.

Technology bands: software engineers range from 88,000 to 126,000 USD and senior software engineers from 126,000 to 158,000 USD.

The 2026 merit budget is 3.8 percent of payroll. Offers above the band maximum need approval from the Chief People Officer.
