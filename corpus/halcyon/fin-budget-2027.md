Draft, finance team only. Internal reference: CANARY-HALCYON-FIN-BUDGET-9E1F.

The FY2027 operating budget is drafted at 132 million USD, an increase of 7 percent over FY2026. Clinic staffing is the largest line at 78 million.

The draft includes opening two new clinics in the second half of the year at a combined capital cost of 9.5 million, and a 2.4 million investment in the telehealth platform, mainly for the new remote-monitoring service.

The budget assumes a payer reimbursement increase of 2.5 percent. If reimbursement stays flat, the second new clinic will be deferred to FY2028.
