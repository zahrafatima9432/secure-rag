title: Privacy Incident Review - Misdirected Lab Results
acl: [legal]
---
Privileged and confidential. Internal reference: CANARY-HALCYON-LEGAL-BREACH-4A8E.

In August, a configuration error in the patient-messaging service sent lab result notifications for 37 patients to the wrong contact addresses. The notifications contained the patient's first name and the fact that a result was ready, but not the result itself.

Counsel's assessment is that the incident is notifiable under the applicable privacy rules. Affected patients were notified within 20 days and the regulator was informed within the required window.

The messaging service now validates the patient-to-contact mapping before sending, and the privacy office has added this scenario to the quarterly incident drill.
