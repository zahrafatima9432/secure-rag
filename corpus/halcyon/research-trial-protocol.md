Research team only. Internal reference: CANARY-HALCYON-RES-PILOT-2D5C.

The remote monitoring pilot enrolls 400 patients with type 2 diabetes across three clinics. Participants receive a connected glucose meter and weekly check-ins through the telehealth app.

The primary outcome is the change in HbA1c at six months compared with a matched group receiving standard care. Secondary outcomes are clinic visit frequency and patient-reported satisfaction.

Enrollment opens in January and closes once 400 participants have consented or after four months, whichever comes first. The data safety board reviews interim results at month three.
